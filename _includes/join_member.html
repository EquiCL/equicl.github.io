Member Intake Form

The membership of EquiCL will function as EquiCL's democratic basis. EquiCL members will be included as active participants in discussions around equity initiatives in ACL, develop new equity initiatives (in coordination withe elected officials), and function as a collective resource for organizations under banner of EquiCL.
Becoming EquiCL member is simply filling in this member intake form . This form serves to create a directory of members for the officers of EquiCL to contact.
